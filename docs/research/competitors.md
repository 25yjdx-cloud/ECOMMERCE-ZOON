# Competitor notes (public homepages seen on 2026-11-15; verify before relying on them)

## Qumra (qumra.cloud)
- Arabic SaaS store builder: dashboard, themes, POS, landing pages, an AI assistant, and integrations.
- Theme cards show: name, rating, short description, industry tags, author, install count, buttons to create a store and to preview a live demo store.
- Payment partners listed: Paymob, PayTabs, Stripe, PayPal. Shipping partners listed: Bosta, Mylerz, Aramex.
- Self-reported: more than 5000 stores. Prices were not shown on the homepage.

## Elmatajer (elmatajer.com)
- Egyptian SaaS: ready templates, a custom domain, cash on delivery and local gateways, shipping to governorates, an AI store-setup assistant, and a 14-day trial without a card.
- Six templates listed: Classic (multi-purpose), Bazaaria, Velora and Farida (fashion), Voltex (electronics), ArtWall (home decor). Each links to a live demo store. Advertised: editable colors and fonts, Arabic and English, light and dark.
- Payments listed: Paymob, Kashier, cash on delivery. Shipping listed: Bosta, Aramex, Mylerz, ShipBlu, J&T Express.
- Prices (monthly, paid monthly; annual billing shown separately): Growth 12.5 USD (listed as 15 struck through); Advanced 24.17 USD (listed as 29 struck through); Professional 49.17 USD (listed as 59 struck through). Annual totals: 150, 290 and 590 USD. The free plan is the 14-day trial. These are the price anchors clients compare against.

## What we borrow
- The gallery card pattern with a live preview per template.
- Arabic and English, RTL and LTR as defaults; light and dark as an optional capability.
- The partner shortlist to verify: Paymob, Kashier (payments); Bosta, Aramex, Mylerz, ShipBlu, J&T Express (shipping).

## What we do not copy
- Their brand, text, images or template designs.
- Self-service SaaS machinery (sign-up, billing, tenant admin): that is Stage B.

## Our angle
A freelance-built, custom store for each client, with its own backend; the client owns the code and the data; no platform commission on orders; a profit and loss dashboard with the cost recorded at the time of sale. Verify each claim against the competitor before using it in a sales pitch.
